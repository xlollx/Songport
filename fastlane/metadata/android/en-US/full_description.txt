<p>Keep your playlists identical across music services, without rebuilding them by hand. Everything runs on your phone: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pick a source playlist and a target one, or let the app create it</li>
<li>Sync by hand or on a schedule: hourly, every 6 hours, daily or weekly</li>
<li>Additions only, or mirror removals too</li>
<li>Liked songs as a source</li>
<li>Review the tracks that were not found: one-tap proposals, the likeliest results first, the source album's tracks, or paste the track's link</li>
<li>Import and export playlist files (CSV, M3U, iTunes XML, JSON) for services without an API</li>
<li>Backups of a whole account as dated versions, duplicate removal</li>
</ul>
<p>Works with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL, Deezer, Subsonic/Navidrome, Jellyfin and Plex servers, Last.fm and ListenBrainz as sources, and files. Services that need a developer key are set up with an in-app guide; the key and every access token stay encrypted on the device.</p>
<p>YouTube Music, Amazon Music and the Spotify and Apple Music sign-in without developer keys work through the services' own web players, built in. They are not official APIs: the app shows a notice before the first sign-in.</p>
<p>Songport is not affiliated with any of these services; their names are used only to indicate compatibility.</p>
