# Getting the word out

The checklist lives in the repository, next to the code:
[docs/ROADMAP.md › Getting the word out](https://github.com/xlollx/Songport/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md#getting-the-word-out).

In short: Obtainium's app list, the apps pages of Navidrome and Jellyfin, awesome lists on GitHub
(offa/android-foss, pcqpcq/open-source-android-apps, awesome-jellyfin), AlternativeTo (as an
alternative to Soundiiz, TuneMyMusic and FreeYourMusic), IzzyOnDroid, and posts on r/fossdroid,
r/androidapps, r/navidrome, r/jellyfin, Lemmy and Mastodon.
