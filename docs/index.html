What it does

Pick a source and a target, choose how often. Trackhop finds every track on the other side, shows you what will change, and keeps both in step.

Any service to any service

Playlists, liked songs, saved albums and followed artists. Or paste the link of any public playlist.

On a schedule

Hourly, every 6 hours, daily or weekly. Additions only, or mirror removals too, and keep the source's order.

Nothing guessed

A preview before every sync. Tracks that were not found go to a review: pick the right one once, Trackhop remembers it.

Your accounts stay yours

You sign in on each service's own page. Access tokens are encrypted on the phone and never leave it.

Your own music server

Navidrome and other Subsonic servers, Jellyfin and Plex, next to the streaming services. Files too: CSV, M3U, XML, JSON.

Playlist tools

Backups, duplicate removal, merge, split and sort, and a playlist generator driven by your own AI key.

Works with

Services marked beta work and follow each service's documented API, but have had less real-world use: reports are welcome.

Why not an online transfer service?

Soundiiz, TuneMyMusic and FreeYourMusic are good at a one-off move. Trackhop is for keeping playlists in step over time, without handing your accounts to anyone. Side by side: Trackhop and Soundiiz, Trackhop and TuneMyMusic.

Also on a computer or a NAS

Want to try it without a phone, or keep syncs running on a machine that is always on? The same engine runs on your computer and on a server, with a web page to connect services and create syncs. Both are a beta: they work for everyday use but are still young, so keep a copy of your set-up and tell us what breaks.

Trackhop Desktop beta for Windows, macOS and Linux: install it like any program, no Docker or Java needed, no administrator rights. It sits next to the clock and runs scheduled syncs while the computer is on. Version 0.6.1 (what's in it).

Docker beta, for a home server, a NAS or a Raspberry Pi:

docker run -d -p 8080:8080 \
  -v trackhop-data:/data \
  -e SONGPORT_PASSWORD=choose-one \
  ghcr.io/xlollx/trackhop

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080. To update, pull the image and start the container again: your syncs stay in the volume. Settings, Docker Compose and what works there: the Docker guide. How Trackhop uses your data

Trackhop connects to the music services you choose (Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, TIDAL, Deezer, personal servers) with each service's own sign-in. It reads your playlists and their tracks, and, when you start or schedule a sync, creates playlists and adds or removes tracks in your own account. For YouTube Music through Google's API this means the YouTube Data API with the youtube permission, used only for your playlists and only on your device.

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the phone and never sent to Trackhop's developer or to any third party. There is no Trackhop server. Details in the privacy policy.
